🏢For letting agents & property managers

Your landlords will
ask you first.

Landlords must start their own registrations on the government's Register your rental property service — but they can invite your agency to add the tenancy details and certificates. That means a unique access link and password for every property, 28-day deadlines on every record, and updates every time a certificate is renewed.

What agents can do on the register
  • ✓HMO, additional or selective licence details
  • ✓Number of tenants and households
  • ✓Furnished, part-furnished or unfurnished
  • ✓Rent, and any bills included
  • ✓Gas safety certificate, EICR or EIC, and EPC — with issue and expiry dates
  • ✓Keep all of the above updated using the same link
⚠️ It's an offence to knowingly or recklessly provide false or misleading information, and landlords are notified of everything you submit. Accuracy matters.
Source: GOV.UK, guidance for letting agents and property managers (29 Oct 2026)
